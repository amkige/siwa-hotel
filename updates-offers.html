  • Q: Do you provide transportation services to and from the airport?

    A: Yes, we offer transportation services to and from the airport for our guests' convenience. Please contact our reception desk in advance to arrange for airport transfers.

    A: Yes, our hotel features a restaurant serving delicious local and international cuisine prepared with fresh ingredients. Guests can also enjoy al fresco dining under the starlit sky or opt for in-room dining for a more intimate experience.

    A: Siwa Oasis is rich in cultural heritage, and there are often traditional festivals and events happening throughout the year. Our concierge desk can provide you with information about upcoming events and assist in planning memorable experiences during your stay.

  • Q: What recreational activities are available at your hotel in Siwa Oasis?

    A: Our hotel offers a range of recreational activities, including guided desert safaris, camel rides, and traditional Siwan cultural experiences. Guests can also relax by our pool or indulge in a rejuvenating spa treatment.
